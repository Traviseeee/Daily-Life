What MYLIFE is

MYLIFE is a personal app for money, bills, events, goals and family memories. We don't sell data, show ads, or track you for advertising.

What we store

  • Account: your email (and your name when you sign in with Google). Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are never stored in plain text.
  • Your app data: what you enter (money, bills, loans, events, goals, family details), kept in your own account on Supabase with a copy on your device.
  • Photos and videos: kept in private storage and shown through short-lived links.
  • PIN / Face ID: stay on your device only (the PIN is hashed).

Who can see it

  • Only you can see your data; this is enforced by the database (Row Level Security).
  • Family: members you invite see only what you allow (for example memories you share). Money and loans are off by default.

Services the app uses

  • Supabase (accounts, database, storage) and Vercel (hosting).
  • Google Sign-In (if you choose it): we receive only your email, name and profile picture.
  • Telegram bot (if you connect it): messages you send to the bot are used to record items and send reminders; the bot's short-lived state is kept in Upstash Redis.
  • Google Gemini AI (only with your own API key): summaries of your data and receipts you scan are sent to Google. With free keys Google may use them to improve its services.
  • Open-Meteo (weather) and Google Fonts.

Export or delete

You can export or back up your data in Settings. To delete your account and all of its data, email chinchetra107@gmail.com.
